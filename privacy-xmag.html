Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Important Safety Notice

xMag is an earthquake monitoring and analysis app that interprets public earthquake catalog data. It is not an official earthquake alert system, earthquake prediction service, safety advice, or emergency service. For emergency instructions and official warnings, follow your local and national authorities.

Overview

xMag is designed to work without creating an account. The app shows live earthquake data, maps, location-based context, saved locations, saved earthquakes, optional push notifications, premium features, and advertising for non-premium users.

Most personal app data, such as saved earthquakes, saved locations, filters, map preferences, and premium entitlement status, is stored locally on your device. Some data is sent to Apple, Google, or the xMag notification service when those features are used, as described below.

Information You Provide or Enable

  • Location: If you grant location permission, xMag may use your device location to sort earthquakes by distance, show nearby events, calculate approximate fault proximity, and provide location-based analysis. Location access is optional.
  • Saved locations: If you save places in the app, their names, coordinates, and filter settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Saved earthquakes: If you bookmark earthquakes, those records are stored locally on your device so you can return to them later.
  • Notification preferences: If you enable earthquake push notifications, xMag stores your notification settings, magnitude filters, quiet hours, and optional near-me alert location settings.

Push Notifications

xMag uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and an xMag notification backend hosted on Cloudflare Workers to deliver optional earthquake notifications.

When notifications are enabled, the app may send the notification backend your APNs device token, generated device identifier, app version, build number, locale, notification preferences, magnitude thresholds, quiet hours, time zone offset, and, if near-me alerts are enabled, latitude and longitude used for matching nearby earthquake events.

This information is used to register your device, apply your notification preferences, send earthquake notifications, avoid duplicate delivery, troubleshoot delivery errors, and disable inactive tokens. You can disable notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.

Location Data

xMag uses location only when you grant permission or choose location-based features. The app may use location locally to calculate distance from earthquakes, show nearby events, estimate whether an event may be relevant to your location, and calculate approximate distance to mapped fault data.

If near-me push notifications are enabled, latitude and longitude may be sent to the xMag notification backend so it can match earthquake events against your selected radius and magnitude settings. If near-me notifications are disabled, location is not needed for that notification matching.

Advertising

xMag uses Google AdMob to show ads to non-premium users. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers when available, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and consent choices to provide, measure, limit, and improve advertising.

Saved earthquakes, saved locations, and local app settings are not shared by xMag with Google for third-party advertising. Premium users may see fewer or no ads depending on the current premium feature set.

You can learn more about Google's use of information from apps that use its services at Google's partner technologies policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

xMag uses Apple's StoreKit system for in-app purchases. Payment processing is handled by Apple. xMag does not receive your full payment card details. The app may store local premium entitlement status so premium features can be unlocked after purchase or restore.

Terms for the app are provided through Apple's standard licensed application end user license agreement unless a custom license is separately published: Apple Standard EULA.

Earthquake Data and Third-Party Sources

xMag reads public earthquake and map-related data from third-party sources such as USGS, EMSC, GEM, and local bundled datasets used for city, fault, map, or earthquake context. These sources provide earthquake catalog or reference data. xMag does not control the privacy practices, availability, or accuracy of those third-party data providers.

Information We Do Not Require

xMag does not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera access, microphone access, health data, or financial account details.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by xMag remains on your device unless you delete it in the app where available, reset related settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app generally removes locally stored app data from the device.

Notification backend records may remain for as long as needed to provide notifications, maintain delivery logs, troubleshoot delivery errors, and prevent duplicate delivery. Disabling notifications or deleting the app prevents future notification registration updates from the app. Apple purchase records are managed by Apple according to Apple's policies.

Your Choices

  • You can enable or disable location permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can enable or disable push notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • You can reset or limit advertising identifiers through iOS privacy settings where available.
  • You can delete saved earthquakes or saved locations in the app where those controls are available.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device.

Children's Privacy

xMag is a general earthquake monitoring and information app. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when xMag changes or when legal, platform, technical, or App Store requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or xMag's privacy practices, please contact us through the support contact provided on the App Store product page.

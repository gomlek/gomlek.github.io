Move your Goodreads library to BooksMy

Export your books from Goodreads and import them into BooksMy in a few minutes. Your library stays on your device; you don’t need a BooksMy account.

1. Export your library from Goodreads

  1. Sign in to Goodreads in a web browser (the export is not available in the Goodreads app).
  2. Open My Books, then choose Import and export in the left column.
  3. Choose Export Library and wait for the file to be created, then download the CSV.
  4. Save the file where your iPhone can reach it, for example in iCloud Drive or by sending it to yourself.

2. Import it into BooksMy

  1. In BooksMy, open Settings › Import / Export.
  2. Choose Import CSV and select the Goodreads file.
  3. Choose the Goodreads format on the Import CSV screen.
  4. Review the preview. You can see rows that match books you already have and rows that are invalid. An invalid row doesn’t change an existing book.
  5. Confirm the import.

What comes across

The Goodreads export doesn’t include reading start dates. For finished books, the start and end dates are imported as the Goodreads reading date. Cover images aren’t downloaded in a Goodreads import.

Free version and the 10-book limit

In the free version the limit is 10 books in total, and importing from Goodreads or CSV above 10 books needs the one-time Pro purchase. Pro removes the limit and the ads. Your existing books are never locked. See About Pro.

Before you start

Your Goodreads account is not touched, and BooksMy has no account of its own. To keep your whole BooksMy library safe afterwards, use the iCloud backup (how backups work).

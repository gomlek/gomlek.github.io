From library to reading journal

Make room for your reading habit.

  • Organize your library

    Add books by barcode, by searching Google Books, or by hand. Reach your books with the Reading, To Read, Finished, and Did Not Finish filters.

  • Record your reading time

    Start a reading session; pause it for a break, then resume. When the session ends, save your time and page progress.

  • Return to your notes

    Keep notes and quotes with page details. You can scan page text or dictate it; review the text before you save it.

  • Follow your goals and pace

    Set daily, monthly, and yearly goals. See your progress in Pace, and your saved reading in the calendar and statistics.

  • Look back on your year

    See the books you finished and the reading time you saved in Year in Review. Make a reading card to share.

  • Give your books a structure

    Create lists and record collection and loan details. Smart Lists gather the books that match the rules you choose.

Your library is on your device. Your backup is in your iCloud.

Your books, notes, and reading records are stored on your device. You can back up to your own iCloud account and restore on another device. The library doesn’t sync automatically between devices.

You can import and export your books as CSV, and import a CSV file from Goodreads.

About backups and moving your data

Free and Pro

Build your library first.

In the free version, the limit for adding books from the BooksMy › + menu is 10 books in total; books in Did Not Finish count too. Your existing books are never locked.

An ad may appear when the app opens. At the limit you can choose to watch a short ad to add each new book. A one-time, lifetime Pro purchase removes the limit and the ads. If you already bought Pro, you don’t need to pay again for the same entitlement.

BooksMy in the classroom

A reading journal that needs no account and keeps each reader’s library on their own device.

Why it suits school use

  • No account. Students don’t sign up for anything and don’t give BooksMy a name or e-mail address.
  • Data stays on the device. Books, notes, and reading records are stored on the iPhone or iPad; the optional backup goes to the reader’s own iCloud. See the privacy page.
  • Reading time and goals. A reading timer with pause and resume, daily, monthly, and yearly goals, notes and quotes with page details.
  • Barcode scanning for adding books to a personal or class library quickly, and import or export by CSV.
  • 34 languages of the interface.

What it costs

BooksMy is free to start: the first 10 books are free, and ads can appear in the free version. A one-time Pro purchase removes the 10-book limit and the ads. There is no subscription. For classrooms we suggest Pro, so students don’t see ads.

Rolling it out

Schools can distribute apps through Apple School Manager and Apple Business Manager. If you want to plan a class set or ask about Pro for a group, write to lezzet@gmail.com and say how many devices you have. BooksMy is a one-person project; replies can take a while.

BooksMy press information

An independent reading journal for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by one developer since 2020.

Facts

  • What it is: a reading journal for iPhone and Apple Watch. Save books, time reading sessions, keep notes and quotes, follow goals.
  • Developer: Tamer Bayramogullari, independent, one person.
  • Since: 2020 (first release April 2020). Version 3.0 is the current major release.
  • Languages: 34.
  • Price: free to start; one-time Pro purchase removes ads and the 10-book limit. No subscription. The price is set in each country’s App Store.
  • Privacy: no account; the library is stored on the device; optional backup to the user’s own iCloud. Privacy page.

What’s new in 3.0

  • Reading timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and on Apple Watch.
  • Notes and quotes per book, with page details; scan or dictate text.
  • Share cards for reading and quotes.
  • Year in Review, Pace, and statistics.
  • Import from Goodreads and CSV, with a preview before anything changes. See the Goodreads guide.
